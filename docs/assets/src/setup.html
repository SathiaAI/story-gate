SETUP · NO CODING

Five steps. Your AI does the work.

You only do what has to be you: one sentence, three clicks and one key. Your AI installs everything else, and a page in your browser shows each step.

1
YOU TYPE

Ask your AI

Set up story-gate in this repo: github.com/SathiaAI/story-gate

In Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Hermes. It installs story-gate and opens the setup page.

2
YOU CLICK

Sign in to GitHub

Click Authorize. This lets story-gate set up your repository.

3
YOU CLICK

Give your AI its own login

Click Create, then Install. Your AI never uses your account.

4
YOU PASTE

Add the judge key

Make a key at openrouter.ai and paste it once. Your AI never sees it.

5
YOU MERGE

Approve the setup

Merge the pull request your AI opened. Then the page says protected.

Your AI does the rest: the files, the checks on every pull request, the branch rules, the dashboard and the protection on your computer.

Free GitHub plan?

On private repositories GitHub only enforces "must be approved" on paid plans. story-gate still checks every pull request and marks it ADVISORY.
